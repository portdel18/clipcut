Media

Drag a card onto the main track to put it between clips, or above it to lay it over them as an overlay. Press + to add it at the end.

Audio

Sound sits on its own lane and stays where you put it when the main track changes. Select it to set its volume and fades.

Transcript

Click a word to go to it. Drag across words (or Shift+click) and press Delete to cut them out of the video.

Transitions

Click the square between two clips on the main track, then pick a transition.

Transitions play over the cut, half in each clip, so the video keeps its length. Where a clip has no footage past its edge, its first or last frame holds.

Filters

Select a clip to give it a filter.

Exposure, contrast, white balance and more are in the clip's Properties, under Adjust.

Mixer

A lane's volume and pan go on top of its clips' own. Solo plays only the soloed lanes. The meters move while the video plays; red means the sound is too loud and will distort.

Text

Text goes above the main track at the playhead, three seconds long. Type in the Properties panel; drag it in the player to move it.

Titles

A title is a ready-made design: change its words and colours in Properties. Save any text as a title of your own with Save as title.
